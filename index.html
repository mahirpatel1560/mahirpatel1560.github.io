02 / 04 — RESEARCH · URSAAUG 2025 – JAN 2026

Magnet

Serving systems execute each LLM call independently — downstream agents wait for complete inputs, serializing the whole pipeline. Magnet treats a multi-agent workflow as a dataflow graph, overlapping downstream context prefill with upstream generation through just-in-time and parallel prefilling.

My work: instrumented multi-agent workloads, collected execution traces — token rates, prefill times, KV-cache memory, dependency structure — and built a trace-driven simulator for just-in-time prefill scheduling, evaluated on latency, throughput, and GPU utilization against sequential baselines.
